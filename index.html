GIS specialist, cartographer and geospatial software developer. He created GIS Development Skills to bring practical engineering guidance, code references, geospatial data services and starter templates into one open resource. The project focuses on building reliable, testable tools for ArcGIS, QGIS, desktop GIS and mapping applications, drawing on lessons from projects such as MIRSAD to support developers, researchers and contributors.
